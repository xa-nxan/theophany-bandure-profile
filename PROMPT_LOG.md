# Prompt Log — About Section Copy

## 1. My prompt

> I'm Theophany Bandure, a second-year Software Engineering student at Africa
> University. I'm writing the "About" section of a one-page personal
> portfolio site for a Web Technologies assignment. Write a short bio (2
> short paragraphs, roughly 60–90 words total) that mentions: (1) my
> programme and year, (2) that I'm currently learning semantic HTML5, CSS3,
> and how to use AI tools responsibly in a development workflow, and (3) a
> genuine, slightly reflective tone rather than a sales pitch. Keep the
> language plain and first-person — no buzzwords like "passionate" or
> "innovative," and no exclamation marks. Format as plain prose, no
> headings or bullet points.

## 2. Raw AI output

> I am a dedicated second-year Software Engineering student at Africa
> University with a strong passion for building innovative digital
> solutions. I am currently expanding my skill set in semantic HTML5, CSS3,
> and exploring how artificial intelligence tools can enhance the modern
> development workflow.
>
> I believe that combining solid technical fundamentals with emerging AI
> technologies is the key to becoming a well-rounded software engineer, and
> I am excited to continue growing on this journey.

## 3. My edited final version

> I'm a second-year Software Engineering student at Africa University,
> currently working through the Web Technologies unit and getting
> comfortable with the idea that good software starts with structure —
> clean markup, clear logic, and interfaces people don't have to think
> twice about.
>
> Right now I split my time between coursework, small personal projects,
> and figuring out how AI tools fit into a real development workflow rather
> than replacing it. This page is one of those experiments: hand-built
> HTML, styled by hand, with an AI-assisted first draft of this bio that I
> rewrote until it actually sounded like me.

## 4. Reflection

The raw output used exactly the buzzwords I asked it to avoid ("passionate,"
"innovative," "excited to continue growing") and read like generic LinkedIn
copy rather than something I'd actually say. I cut every stock phrase,
replaced the vague "expanding my skill set" with a concrete idea (structure,
clean markup), and added a specific, honest detail — that this very page is
an example of AI-assisted-but-human-edited work — which gives the bio a
point of view the original didn't have.
